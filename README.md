# About

I am a Computer Science student at the University of Calgary focused on building scalable systems and performance-oriented software.

Currently working as a **Research Assistant** in database systems and infrastructure, focusing on benchmarking, low-level programming and systems engineering.

Before university, I built large-scale Minecraft server systems, including over 10 production plugins and a complete multi-team competitive gamemode. This experience sparked my interest in distributed systems, networking and performance-oriented design.

Alongside my technical work, I am building an online course that introduces Spanish-speaking students to computer science through Minecraft plugin development with Kotlin, with the goal of creating accessible pathways into advanced computing.
